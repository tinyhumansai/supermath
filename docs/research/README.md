# Research

Notes on outside work that informs how this project is built: other
repositories, papers, and techniques studied before or during design. Research
notes are not specifications. They record what was observed and what we took
from it; a decision that follows from them belongs in a spec or an ADR, with a
link back here.

## Index

- **[`rea-and-jacobian/`](rea-and-jacobian/README.md)**: how the author of
  [morluto/rea](https://github.com/morluto/rea) (a reverse-engineering MCP
  server) and [morluto/jacobian](https://github.com/morluto/jacobian) (a
  mathematics MCP server) approaches building hard, wide-surface tools for
  agents, and what that means for supermath.

## Conventions

- Pin every external source to a revision and a date. An unpinned observation
  goes stale without anyone noticing.
- Keep what the source says separate from our reading of it. Mark inferences
  as inferences.
- Keep each file at 500 lines or fewer, as with the rest of `docs/`.
